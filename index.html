01 / APPROACH

Observation
becomes action.

Designing experimental systems in which measurement, imaging and computation can become active components of the experiment.

I build systems that observe, characterize and act on evolving physical systems.

My work sits at the interface between scientific questions and experimental engineering.

I combine instrumentation, electronics, mechanics, optics, software, imaging and computation to create experimental architectures adapted to the phenomena being studied.

Real-time images and sensor data can become active variables: information is acquired, analysed, interpreted and used to modify the experiment.

The objective is to develop dynamic experimental functions that can be transferred, replicated and adapted to different scientific applications.
