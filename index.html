01 / APPROACH

Observation
becomes action.

An experimental system can do more than measure. It can analyse information in real time and use it to dynamically modify the experiment.

I build systems that observe, characterize and act on evolving physical systems.

My work combines scientific instrumentation, electronics, mechanics, optics, software, imaging and computation.

Real-time images and sensor data become active experimental variables. Information extracted from an observation can trigger a decision and modify the experimental conditions.

This creates experimental functions based on acquisition, analysis, decision and action, implemented on digital, embedded or more complex architectures.

The objective is to develop architectures that can be transferred, replicated and adapted to different scientific applications.
