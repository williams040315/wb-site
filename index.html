01 / PRINCIPLE

From observation
to dynamic control.

Experimental information becomes an active component of the experiment itself.

I design systems that observe, characterize and act on the phenomena they study.

The experimental system is considered as an integrated architecture combining measurement, computation and control.

Real-time imaging, sensor data, signal processing, computer vision, artificial intelligence and embedded systems can be combined to extract information from an evolving physical system.

This information can then be used to dynamically adapt the experimental conditions, creating new functions of measurement, characterization and control.
